# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard — Power BI

### Data-Driven Customer Retention & Behavioral Analytics

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer churn behavior, identify high-risk segments, and provide actionable business insights to support retention strategies.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Customer churn significantly impacts subscription-based business revenue.  
This project transforms raw customer data into an executive-ready analytics dashboard that enables stakeholders to understand **who churns, why they churn, and how churn risk evolves across customer lifecycle stages**.

The dashboard combines demographic, geographic, behavioral, and financial analysis into a unified decision-support system.

## 🔎 Analytical Modules

### 🧠 Churn Root Cause Analysis
- Competitor offerings identified as primary churn driver
- Price sensitivity and service dissatisfaction highlighted
- Support experience and network reliability impacts analyzed

---

### 🌎 Geographic Churn Intelligence
- State-level churn comparison using map visualization
- Regional churn concentration analysis
- Location-based customer risk identification

---

### 👥 Demographic Risk Profiling
- Senior customers showed highest churn probability
- Age-bin segmentation revealed vulnerable customer groups
- Customer lifecycle differences identified

---

### 💰 Revenue & Billing Behavior
- Higher monthly charges correlated with increased churn
- Usage segmentation exposed high-risk billing cohorts

---

### 📑 Contract Impact Analysis
- Month-to-month contracts showed significantly higher churn (~46%)
- Long-term contracts demonstrated strong retention behavior

---

### 📶 Service & Usage Analytics
- Unlimited data plan customers exhibited higher churn exposure
- Consumption-based segmentation enabled behavioral comparison

---

### ⏳ Customer Lifecycle Analysis
- Early tenure customers more likely to churn
- Payment method patterns linked to retention outcomes
- Contract type and tenure interaction analysis
